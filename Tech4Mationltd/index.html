Who We Are

About Tech4mation

Multi-faceted Indigenous Information Technology Company based in Lagos State, Nigeria

14+ Years of Excellence

We Care About Your Business

Tech4mation LTD is a multi-faceted indigenous Information Technology Company established in 2010, with branches in Abuja and Anambra, Nigeria. Our areas of competence include Telecom infrastructure deployment and support, Web and mobile App development, Telecoms Consulting, and I.T support.

Since 2010, our platform has enabled over 25 communication service providers across Nigeria and West Africa to offer and monetize multiple telecom services. We help businesses elevate their value through custom software development, product design, QA and consultancy services.
